Lifetime Savings/Investment Account Simulator

Compare UK ISA, Canada TFSA, Sweden ISK, Norway shielding-deduction, and an illustrative EU wrapper against the Irish taxable baseline.

UK ISA

Fixed annual allowance, fully tax-free growth. No carry-forward of unused allowance across tax years. Flexible ISA withdrawals only restore room within the same tax year — not modelled here.

Canada TFSA

Annual room accumulates & carries forward across years. Withdrawals restore room the following calendar year. Tax-free inside wrapper.

Sweden ISK

No contribution limit. Annual notional tax on the kapitalunderlag (sum of 4 quarter-opening balances + deposits, divided by 4). From 2025 a SEK 150k (~€13k) threshold is tax-free.

Norway shielding

Tax-free skjermingsfradrag compounds annually on cost basis at the shielding rate. Only realised gains above accumulated shielding are taxed (at standard CGT/income rate). Unused shielding carries forward.
Illustrative only. No harmonised EU tax treatment exists. Parameters below are invented to explore a partial-exemption design space — do not cite as a real proposal.
